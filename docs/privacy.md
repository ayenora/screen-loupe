# Privacy Policy

Screen Loupe collects no data.

- **Screen Recording.** The app asks for Screen Recording access so it can show the part of the screen inside its Capture Area. The captured image is shown in the app's own window and never leaves your Mac.
- **No network.** The app sends nothing anywhere: no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports, no account. The only links it opens are the guide on this website, in your browser, when you choose Help.
- **What stays on your Mac.** Your settings, the recent colours you pin, and the reference images you add to the project are kept on your Mac, in the app's own sandbox container. Screenshots are saved only where you choose. Recent captures are kept in memory and are gone when the app quits.
- **Sandboxed.** The app runs in the macOS App Sandbox with no network access and no access to your files beyond the ones you pick in an open or save dialog. You can check this yourself: `codesign -d --entitlements - /Applications/ScreenLoupe.app`.
- **Third parties.** The app contains no third-party code or services.

Questions: open an issue at [github.com/ayenora/screen-loupe](https://github.com/ayenora/screen-loupe/issues).

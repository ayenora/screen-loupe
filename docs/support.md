# Support

[Home](.) · [Guide](guide)

Most answers are in the [guide](guide), and its last section covers the usual Screen Recording problems.

- **Questions and ideas:** ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/ayenora/screen-loupe/discussions) — how to do something, what you'd like the app to do, what you made with it.
- **Bugs:** open an [issue](https://github.com/ayenora/screen-loupe/issues).

## A good bug report

- **The app's version and build:** Screen Loupe › About Screen Loupe shows both, and which build it is.
- **Your macOS version:** Apple menu › About This Mac.
- **Your displays:** how many, which are Retina, and how they are arranged if the bug involves more than one.
- **What you did, what you expected and what happened** — step by step, so it can be repeated. A screenshot or a short screen recording helps.
